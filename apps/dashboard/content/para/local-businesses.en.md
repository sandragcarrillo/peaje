# Peaje for local businesses: help AI know where you are and recommend you in your city

When someone asks ChatGPT for a dentist nearby or a mechanic they can trust, the assistant searches the web and names two or three places. To name you, it has to know what you do, which neighborhood you are in and that you are a real business. It gets that from your site and from what directories, lists and forums say about you. Peaje checks both, measures whether you get named in your city and gets the fixes ready.

## What people in your city ask AI

The questions almost always include a place:

- Pediatric dentist near downtown Austin open on Saturdays
- Which hair salon in Miami is good for curly hair?
- Vegetarian restaurant in Mexico City to go with friends
- Which auto repair shop in Toronto is trustworthy for brakes?
- 24 hour vet in Lima, which one do you recommend?
- Where can adults take swimming lessons in Santiago?
- Which physical therapist in Denver treats running injuries?

In the free Radar you pick your customers' country and, if you want, your city. With a city, the three questions it writes name it or say "near me", the way someone there would type them, and ChatGPT and Claude get them with that location. Then you see who they name instead of you.

## What Peaje checks on a local business site

- **Whether your contact details and address are in your page's code (JSON-LD).** AI uses them to confirm you are a real business before recommending you.
- **Whether your data links to your official profiles.** Instagram, Google Business Profile or LinkedIn confirm to AI that all those mentions are yours.
- **Whether you have about, contact and privacy pages.** Agents check them before recommending you.
- **Whether your site comes up first when people search your name.** If it does not, an agent can mix you up with a business with a similar name.
- **Whether your data marks services, reviews or events.** That lets AI answer more kinds of questions about you.
- **Whether your page shows without JavaScript and whether your robots.txt lets AI search bots in.** Many sites made with page builders fail here without the owner knowing.

## Which fixes your agent gets ready

- **Your details on the homepage.** In the free kit you fill in your logo, phone, address, a short description and your profiles. Peaje puts them in your homepage code, and only adds what you fill in.
- **The same name and address everywhere.** The kit gives you the list of Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places and LinkedIn with your name and address ready to copy, so they read the same in every place.
- **Getting named on the pages AI already reads.** If AI answers "24 hour vet in Lima" by citing a directory or a blog list, your agent drafts a short, honest request to be included, with one fact anyone can check on your site. You send it. On a forum, the task is to answer the question and say you work there. It never pays to be listed, never writes reviews of your business and never asks anyone for a review they did not write from their own experience.
- **A page for each question where you do not show up.** With Pro that is up to {{paginas}} drafts a month, written with your site's facts. If a fact is missing, it asks you instead of making it up.
- **Letting the citing bots in**, if your robots.txt or your firewall stops them.

## What Peaje does not do

- It only measures ChatGPT and Claude. It does not measure Gemini, Perplexity, Google Maps or Google's AI Overviews.
- It does not manage your Google Business Profile or your reviews. It tells you where your details have to match and you update them.
- The kit does not put your opening hours in your homepage data. Write your hours as text on your contact page so AI can read them.
- It does not check whether what AI says about your business is correct. You check that by asking ChatGPT and Claude.
- We do not guarantee you will be recommended. Peaje measures again 2 and 6 weeks after each fix so you can see whether it changed.
