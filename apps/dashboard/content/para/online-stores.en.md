# Peaje for online stores: get ChatGPT and Claude to find your products and your prices

More and more people ask ChatGPT or Claude where to buy something. The assistant searches the web, reads a handful of pages and names two or three stores. If it cannot read your product pages or find your prices, it recommends someone else. Peaje checks what an assistant sees when it lands on your store, measures whether it names you when someone looks for what you sell, and gets the fixes ready for you to publish.

## What your shoppers ask AI

Nobody types your store's name. They type what they need:

- Where can I buy whole bean specialty coffee with nationwide shipping?
- Which online store sells authentic running shoes in Canada?
- Best sustainable women's clothing stores with free shipping
- Where do I get bike parts delivered within two days?
- Which store sells cruelty-free natural skincare at a fair price?
- Gift for someone who loves cooking, under 50 dollars
- Which online stores offer free returns within 30 days?

The free Radar writes three questions like these from what your site sells, asks ChatGPT and Claude, and shows you which stores and pages they cite instead of you. If you tell it where your customers are, it writes them the way someone in that country would.

## What Peaje checks on a store

These are the Radar checks that matter most for a store:

- **Whether your prices show.** If an agent cannot find what something costs, it cannot recommend you for a purchase.
- **Whether your product pages show without JavaScript.** Many stores build the page in the browser. Most AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, so they see a blank page.
- **Whether your bot protection lets AI search bots through.** This is common on stores behind Cloudflare: the setting that blocks AI bots also stops the ones that cite.
- **Whether your robots.txt blocks ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity.** If it does, they cannot cite you with a link.
- **Whether your pages carry their preview image and basic data (Open Graph).** Without them, AI can cite your product wrong.
- **Whether your sitemap says when each page changed.** In a store where prices and stock move, that tells crawlers what to read again.
- **Whether you have about, contact and privacy pages.** Agents look for them to confirm you are a real store.

You get your score today, how far it can go and where to start.

## Which fixes your agent gets ready

With Pro, your agent tracks {{preguntas}} shopping questions for your store in ChatGPT and Claude and turns each fix into a task:

- **A page for each question where you do not show up.** If AI answers "best sustainable clothing stores" by citing "best of" lists, the draft takes that shape: clear criteria, your store listed honestly among the options, and a table. It uses only facts from your site, with at least one real number (a price, a shipping time) and a visible date. That is up to {{paginas}} drafts a month.
- **Letting the citing bots in.** If your robots.txt or your firewall blocks the ChatGPT or Claude bot, the task says which line to add or which setting to turn off.
- **Refreshing old pages.** If a size guide or a shipping page shows no date or is months old, the task asks you to check prices and facts and add the update date.
- **Getting named elsewhere.** When AI cites a third-party list, a review blog or a forum for a question where you are missing, the agent drafts a short inclusion request. You send it. It never pays to be listed and never asks for fake reviews.
- **If you connect Search Console:** rewriting the title of a product page Google shows and nobody clicks, or improving one that fell behind.

The free kit puts your store's details on the homepage (name, logo, phone, address, profiles), a guide for agents (llms.txt) and a robots.txt that lets AI search bots in.

## What Peaje does not do

- It only measures ChatGPT and Claude. It does not measure Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews.
- The kit does not write Product schema for each product page. It puts your business details on the homepage. Product data comes from your platform or whoever runs your store.
- The kit command recognizes sites built with Next.js, Nuxt, Astro, SvelteKit, Remix, React Router, Hono, Express and Vite. If your store runs on another platform, the kit lands in a folder with the pieces to add by hand, and what you can add depends on what your platform lets you edit.
- Your agent does not touch your store. It prepares each change and you say yes. You, your developer or your coding tool publish it.
- We do not guarantee you will be cited. Peaje measures each fix again at 2 and 6 weeks, and that shows whether it worked.
