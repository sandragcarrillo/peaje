# Peaje for agencies: one agent per client that gets the fixes ready for your team

Your clients already ask why ChatGPT recommends their competitors and not them. To answer with data you need to measure their questions, know what to change on each site and show whether it worked. With Peaje, each client gets its own agent: it measures their business's questions in ChatGPT and Claude, turns each fix into a task and measures again at 2 and 6 weeks. Your team does the work and your client approves it.

## How it works with several clients

- **One email, several sites.** You sign in with your email and register one site per client from "Your sites". Each one has its own dashboard, tasks and measurements, kept apart.
- **One connection per site.** Each site has its own MCP. Your team connects Claude Code or Cursor in that client's repo and the tool only sees that site's tasks.
- **The agent where you already work.** Each site's agent connects to Telegram, Slack, the dashboard or your AI assistant, and answers with that site's data.
- **A weekly report per site**, plus the daily monitor that alerts you when something breaks.

## What people looking for an agency ask AI

Your agency has its own questions too. You can register your site as one more:

- Which digital marketing agency in Bogotá works with small businesses?
- Agency that builds websites for restaurants in Latin America
- Who can help me get ChatGPT to recommend my business?
- Best SEO agencies for online stores in Spanish-speaking markets
- Which agency runs social media and ads for clinics?
- Web development agency that works with Next.js and has good references

For each client, Peaje writes questions like these from what their site sells and in their buyers' country. The free Radar lets you see it with any site before you pitch it.

## What your team gets for each client

- **Technical fixes with acceptance criteria.** A robots.txt that blocks the ChatGPT or Claude bot, a firewall that stops them, a homepage that is empty without JavaScript, business details missing from the homepage. The monitor checks the site every day and closes the task when the check passes.
- **Page drafts for the questions where the client does not show up.** Up to {{paginas}} a month per site, in the page shape AI cites for that question (guide, list of options, comparison, pricing page) and only with facts from the site. If a fact is missing, the agent asks.
- **Requests to get the client named elsewhere.** The lists, directories and forums AI cites instead, with a short draft for each one. The client sends it, or your team does with their permission. Nobody pays to be listed and nobody asks for fake reviews.
- **Search Console, if the client connects it.** Titles nobody clicks and pages that fell behind.
- **The before and after.** Each fix is measured again at 2 and 6 weeks, with the same questions. That is what you show your client.

## What Peaje does not do

- It only measures ChatGPT and Claude. If your client needs Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews, Peaje does not cover them.
- The subscription is per site. Ten clients on Pro are ten subscriptions.
- Each site belongs to the email account that registered it. Today there are no team seats, no white label and no report that rolls up all your clients.
- It tracks {{preguntas}} questions per site. For brands in many markets, that falls short.
- The agent publishes nothing. Your team applies each change once the client says yes.
- We do not guarantee your clients will be cited. The measurement tells you whether it is happening.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there agency pricing?

There is no separate plan. Each site on Pro pays the same: US${{founder}} a month at the founder price for the first {{cupos}} businesses, and US${{pro}} a month after that. The Radar and the kit are free for all your clients.

### Can I try it with a client before paying?

Yes. The Radar measures any site for free, with no account and no card. When you register the site you get a free pack of {{regaloInformes}} reports and {{regaloRespuestas}} agent answers, once per site.

### Does my client have to log in?

No. You can run the site from your account and show them the report and the measurements. They still approve changes before they go live, because it is their site.

### What happens if a client leaves?

You cancel that site's subscription from its dashboard. It keeps Pro until the end of the paid period and what the agent already delivered stays there. Your other sites do not change.

### Does Peaje replace my team?

No. Peaje measures and prepares the tasks. Your team decides, applies the changes and explains them to the client. That is what the task with criteria is for: the work is clear, and Peaje checks the live site to confirm it is done.
