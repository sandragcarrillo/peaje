# How much does Peaje cost and what do the 500 monthly credits include?

Peaje Pro costs US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses that subscribe, and US$69 after that. It comes with 500 credits a month for your agent's work, which cover, for example, 40 pages, 2 reports and 20 lookups in your data, and it also measures 20 of your customers' questions in ChatGPT and Claude every week. The radar, the kit and priced routes are free, with no card.

Updated: November 2026

**US$30 a month, 500 credits a month,** according to the [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios). Talking to your agent uses no credits.

## How many credits does each job use?

| Work | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| A lookup in your data | 2 |
| A fix | 8 |
| A page draft | 10 |
| A report you ask for | 30 |
| A mystery shopper test | 30 |
| A series of pages | 40 |

## How far do 500 credits go in a month?

It depends on what you use them for. Some combinations:

| If you spend the month on | It covers |
| --- | --- |
| What the plan comes with | 40 page drafts, 2 reports and 20 lookups |
| Content only | 50 page drafts |
| Fixes only | 62 fixes |
| Questions about your data only | 250 lookups |
| Pages in series | 12 series of pages |

You split them however you want among the jobs in the first table.

## What uses no credits?

- Talking to your agent, in the dashboard, on Telegram or from your AI tool.
- The 20 customer questions your agent measures every week in ChatGPT and Claude, which are a separate limit.
- Scheduled reports, like the weekly report.
- The daily monitor of your site and the tasks it leaves when something breaks.

## What happens if I run out of credits?

Your agent keeps talking to you, but it does no new work until the 1st of the next month, which is when they renew. What it already delivered stays in your dashboard. Credits you do not use do not roll over, and you cannot buy extra credits, so there are no surprise charges.

## Does the US$30 price stay?

Yes, as long as your subscription stays active. It is the founder price for the first 50 businesses, and after those 50, Pro costs US$69 a month. If you cancel and come back, you pay the regular price.

## How do I pay and what happens if I cancel?

You pay by card through Paddle, the merchant of record for Peaje's orders: it charges you, calculates your country's taxes and sends you the receipt and the invoice. The price with taxes shows up before you confirm.

- **There is no free trial.** If Pro is not for you, we refund your first payment in full if you ask within 7 days. Renewals are not refunded.
- **You cancel whenever you want,** from your dashboard and with no calls. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, you are not charged again and your site goes back to the free plan.

If you decide to charge AI agents, Peaje keeps 2% of each payment, separate from the subscription ([how it works](https://usepeaje.com/blog/how-much-does-peaje-keep-from-each-ai-agent-payment)). What the free plan includes is in [what does Peaje's free plan include and what does it not](https://usepeaje.com/blog/what-does-peajes-free-plan-include-and-what-does-it-not).
