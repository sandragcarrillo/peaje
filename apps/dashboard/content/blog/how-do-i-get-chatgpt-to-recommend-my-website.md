## Why doesn't ChatGPT recommend your site today?

Because it doesn't understand what you sell. An agent lands, reads your pages and leaves without knowing what you offer, what it costs or how to get started. So it recommends someone who explains it more clearly. Agents need three things from you: to find you, to use you and to buy from you. Most sites fail at the first one.
