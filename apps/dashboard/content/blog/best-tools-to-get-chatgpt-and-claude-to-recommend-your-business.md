# Best tools to get ChatGPT and Claude to recommend your business

There is no single tool that works for everyone, there are three kinds and the best one is the one that fits what you are missing today. If you do not know how AI sees you, start with a free radar; if your site does not have the files agents read, you need a technical kit; and if you want someone to write the pages and measure again, you need an agent. Peaje does all three: the radar and the kit are free, and the agent costs US$30 a month at the founder price.

Updated: October 2026

Peaje's Pro plan costs US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses that subscribe, then US$69, and includes 500 credits a month and 20 tracked questions in AI assistants, according to the [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios).

## What criteria did we use for this list?

- **Public price:** you can see what it costs before talking to sales.
- **Try without a card:** you can see a result before you pay.
- **What it leaves you:** a score, a file ready to upload or a written page.
- **All three parts:** it helps AI find you, helps an agent use your site and, if you sell something, helps it buy from you.

We do not list other tools' prices because they change often and we do not control them, so check them on each tool's own page.

## Radar: the first step

It tells you in under a minute what an agent understands about your site and what it does not. The good: it is free, needs no account and you keep the result. The bad: it does not change anything on its own. In the example on the radar page, agents understand 14 of 29 things, and the site goes from 46 to about 74 with the fixes.

## Technical kit: the files agents read

It generates llms.txt, openapi.json, an MCP server, agent cards and JSON-LD for your site, and you get it by registering your site at [usepeaje.com/nuevo](https://usepeaje.com/nuevo). From your dashboard you install it by pasting a text into your AI coding tool or with `npx @peaje/cli@1 init <slug>`. The good: it is free and does not expire, and it comes with a daily monitor and the agent visit log. The bad: someone has to paste the text or run the command, then deploy, and it does not write content.

## Agent: measures, writes and measures again

The Pro agent builds a plan around your goal, measures whether ChatGPT and Claude cite you, writes full drafts of the pages you are missing with facts from your site, leaves each task for your coding tool through MCP or the CLI and measures again at 2 and 6 weeks. The good: everything in one place, no meetings, with a report every week. The bad: you get 500 credits a month, and if they run out your agent keeps talking to you but does no new work until the 1st.

## What if it does not work for me?

You cancel anytime from the dashboard, with no calls, and you keep your agent until the end of the month you already paid for. If it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full, and worst case you keep your diagnosis and the fixes your agent already handed you.
