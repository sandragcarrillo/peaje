# Peaje vs Otterly vs Peec vs Profound: which one should you pick?

It depends on what you need: if you have a marketing team and want to track your brand across many AI assistants at once, Otterly, Peec and Profound cover more engines and more questions than Peaje. If you run a small business and what you are missing is someone who gets your site's fixes ready, checks they went live and measures again, that is what [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) is built for. Today Peaje tracks only ChatGPT and Claude.

Updated November 2026. The Otterly, Peec and Profound figures come from their pricing pages, checked on November 22, 2026.

Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price for the first 50 businesses, and US$69 after that, with 500 credits a month ([Peaje pricing](https://usepeaje.com/precios)). Otterly starts at €29 a month ([Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing)), Peec at US$95 a month ([Peec pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing)), and Profound only sells Enterprise plans with custom pricing ([Profound pricing](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing)).

## Where are Otterly, Peec and Profound stronger?

- **They track more assistants.** If you care about showing up in Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews, any of the three will track it, and Peaje does not.
- **They track more questions.** Peec starts at 50, Otterly goes up to 403 on Premium, and Profound sizes it to your case, while Peaje tracks 20.
- **They are built for teams.** All three have unlimited users. Otterly covers unlimited brands, competitors, countries and languages on every plan, and Peec lets you work across several countries from its Pro plan up.
- **They show more agent traffic.** Peec tells you which AI bots hit which pages and how many visits arrive from assistants. Profound connects its agent analytics to Cloudflare, Vercel, WordPress, AWS and other platforms. Peaje also keeps a log of agent visits to your site, for free.
- **They have tools for stores.** Peec tracks your products one by one in shopping answers, and Profound tracks ChatGPT Shopping.
- **They have more reporting.** Peec includes a visibility impact analysis and shareable dashboards, and Profound has agents that write content for large teams.

## What does Peaje do that the others do not?

Otterly and Peec also tell you what to change, with audits, recommendations and actions. What their pricing pages do not show is someone checking on your site that the change went live. Peaje does the work end to end:

- **It writes the pages you are missing.** Up to 40 drafts a month, from the facts on your own site, and it asks you what it could not find instead of making it up.
- **It leaves each fix as a task** with its acceptance criteria. Claude Code or Cursor pick it up from Peaje over MCP or the CLI, or you hand it to whoever runs your website.
- **It checks your live site.** When you mark a task done, Peaje visits your site and closes it only if the change is published.
- **It measures each page again at 2 and 6 weeks,** to compare it with your starting point in ChatGPT and Claude.
- **It talks with you in your dashboard, on Telegram or from your AI tool,** and sends you a report every week. The daily monitor, the fix tasks and the weekly report have no limit.

## Can you try it before paying?

With Peaje you can: the [free radar](https://usepeaje.com/radar) checks your site in under a minute, with just your email and no card, and shows your score today, what fails and how far it can go. The kit is free too: llms.txt, openapi.json, an MCP server, agent cards and JSON-LD made for your site. Pro has no free trial, but if it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full.

Otterly and Peec offer a free trial on their pricing pages, though neither says how long it lasts. Profound asks you to book a demo.

## Does one subscription cover several sites?

On Peaje, no: the subscription is per site, according to the [terms](https://usepeaje.com/terms). You can register several sites with the same account, and each one gets its own score. Otterly includes 1 workspace on Lite and unlimited workspaces from Standard up. Peec includes 1 project on Starter, 2 on Pro and 5 on Advanced.

## Do you need a developer?

No. Your agent explains each fix in plain words, writes it and leaves it as a task. If you use Claude Code or Cursor, they pick it up from Peaje. If not, you hand it to whoever runs your website, and Peaje checks the live site afterwards. To install the kit from the terminal, one command is enough: `npx @peaje/cli@1 init <slug>`.

## What if Peaje does not work for you?

Cancel anytime from your dashboard, with no calls, and you keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for. You keep your diagnosis and the fixes your agent already handed you. If you run out of credits, your agent keeps talking with you but does no new work until the 1st of next month, and nothing extra is charged.
