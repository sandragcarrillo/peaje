# Which 20 customer questions should I track in ChatGPT and Claude?

Track the questions your customers ask before they buy, written the way they write them and without your brand name: what options exist, what it costs, which one beats another, how to solve their problem and whether they can trust you. ChatGPT and Claude answer those in your place, and being cited there is what shows up in sales. Split them into five groups of 4 and measure them every week with the same wording.

## How do I split the 20 questions?

| Group | How many | What the customer asks | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Options | 4 | What exists for what they need | What options are there for [what you sell] in [your country]? |
| Price | 4 | What it costs and what is included | How much does [your service] cost in [your city]? |
| Decision | 4 | Which one to pick between two or three | Alternatives to [your best-known competitor]? |
| Problem | 4 | How to solve something specific | How do I [the task your product solves]? |
| Trust | 4 | What happens if something goes wrong | Which [your category] refunds you if it does not work for you? |

When Peaje picks your questions, at least one in five is a decision question ("alternatives to X", "X or Y", "the best [category] for [kind of customer]"). Those are the ones someone asks when they are about to buy.

## How do I pick mine?

1. Open your inbox, your WhatsApp and your sales chat. Copy the questions exactly as people write them.
2. Take your brand out of each one. If it says "does [your company] ship to Austin?", turn it into "which [your category] stores ship to Austin?".
3. Write each one as a single sentence, the way people type into ChatGPT, in under 15 words. No loose keywords.
4. Drop the ones that do not lead to buying from you. If someone can get the answer and never need you, cut it.
5. Sort them into the five groups above until you have 20.

If you do not want to build them by hand, your Peaje agent picks them itself from the facts on your site and your goal when it builds your plan. Then you adjust them.

## Which questions can I copy today?

Change what is in brackets and paste them into ChatGPT and Claude, with search turned on and in a new conversation each time:

- What is the best [your category] for [your kind of customer]?
- How much does [your service] cost in [your city or country]?
- What alternatives are there to [your best-known competitor]?
- [Competitor A] or [competitor B]: which one is better for [your case]?
- How do I fix [the problem you solve] without [what your customer wants to avoid]?
- Which [your category] lets me cancel anytime?

Write down who gets cited in each one. That is your starting point, and also your list of real competitors in AI answers.

## Why do they leave out my brand name?

Because if the question names you, the answer names you. What you want to know is whether ChatGPT and Claude bring you up on their own when someone looks for what you sell, so the questions Peaje measures leave out the business name.

Questions with your brand are useful for something else: checking by hand that they know what you sell and at what price.
