# What does Peaje's free plan include and what does it not?

The free plan includes the radar, the kit with the files agents read (llms.txt, openapi.json, an MCP server, agent cards and JSON-LD), the log of agent visits, a daily monitor with tasks to fix what fails and priced routes to charge AI agents. It asks for no card and it does not expire. What it does not include is your agent: measuring whether ChatGPT and Claude cite you, writing your pages and sending tasks to your coding tool are part of Pro.

Updated: November 2026

Pro costs US$30 a month at the founder price for the first 50 businesses, and US$69 after that, according to the [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios).

## What comes with each plan?

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radar with your score and what fails | Yes | Yes |
| Kit: llms.txt, openapi.json, MCP server, agent cards and JSON-LD | Yes | Yes |
| Log of agent visits | Yes | Yes |
| Daily monitor and fix tasks | Yes | Yes |
| Priced routes and links that answer 402 | Yes, with 2% per payment | Yes, with 2% per payment |
| Your agent in the dashboard and on Telegram | No | Yes |
| Customer questions measured in ChatGPT and Claude | No | 20 |
| Pages written with your site's data | No | 40 a month |
| New measurement at 2 and 6 weeks | No | Yes |
| Tasks in your coding tool, through MCP or the CLI | No | Yes |
| Weekly report | No | Yes |
| Credits per month | 0 | 500 |

## What can I do without paying?

You can do quite a lot if you or someone on your team makes the changes. The [radar](https://usepeaje.com/radar) tells you what fails, the kit generates the files for your site and you install it from your dashboard with a text for your AI coding tool or with a command. The visit log shows which AI bots read your site, and the monitor checks your site every day and leaves you tasks when something stops working. You can also put a price on an API route or a link and charge AI agents, and in that case Peaje keeps 2% of each payment ([how it works](https://usepeaje.com/blog/how-does-an-ai-agent-pay-my-website-and-where-does-the-money-go)). If you do not charge, that 2% does not apply.

## What does it not include?

It does not include tracking your citations. The radar shows you a snapshot of that day with up to 3 questions, and the weekly tracking of 20 questions in ChatGPT and Claude is part of Pro, like the agent that tells you what to publish, writes drafts with your site's data and measures each page again at 2 and 6 weeks. It does not include Integrations either, which is what sends each task to Claude Code, Cursor or Codex and checks it on your site when you publish it ([how it works](https://usepeaje.com/blog/how-do-i-send-my-peaje-tasks-to-claude-code-or-cursor-through-mcp)).

## How do Pro credits work?

Pro comes with 500 credits a month for your agent's work, and talking to it uses none:

| Work | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| A lookup in your data | 2 |
| A fix | 8 |
| A page draft | 10 |
| A report you ask for | 30 |
| A series of pages | 40 |

Credits renew on the 1st of each month, the ones you do not use do not roll over and you cannot buy extra. If you run out, your agent keeps talking to you, but it does no new work until they renew.

## When does upgrading to Pro make sense?

When you already installed the kit and want to know whether it moved anything in AI answers, or when you do not have time to write the missing pages. If you have not installed the kit yet, start there, because it is free and raises your score without paying anything.

There is no free trial of Pro. If you subscribe and it does not work for you, we refund your first payment in full if you ask within 7 days, and you can cancel whenever you want from your dashboard, with no calls. If you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for, your site goes back to the free plan and what your agent already delivered stays in your dashboard. The founder price stays while your subscription is active, and if you cancel and come back, you pay the regular price.

## How do I start?

Register your site at [usepeaje.com/nuevo](https://usepeaje.com/nuevo), with no card. If you want to see where you stand first, run your site through the [radar](https://usepeaje.com/radar), which only asks for your email and your domain.
