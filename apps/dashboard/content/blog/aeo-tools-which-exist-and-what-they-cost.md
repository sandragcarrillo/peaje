# AEO tools: which ones exist and what do they cost?

There are several AEO tools, and they fall into three kinds: radars that measure how an AI sees you, trackers that check whether ChatGPT and Claude cite you, and agents that also write and fix the pages you are missing. Peaje is one of them. It does all three and works in Spanish and English: the radar and the kit are free, and the agent costs US$30 a month (the founder price for the first 50 businesses, then US$69). Other tools' prices change and we do not set them, so here we only give Peaje's.

Updated: October 2026

Peaje's Pro plan includes 500 credits a month for the agent's work and 20 tracked questions in AI assistants, according to our [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios), and talking to your agent uses no credits.

## What kinds of AEO tools exist?

There are three, and it helps not to mix them up:

- **Radar or diagnosis:** looks at your site the way an agent does and tells you what it does not understand. The [Peaje radar](https://usepeaje.com/radar) gives you your score in under a minute, and in the example on the site agents understand 14 of 29 things.
- **Citation tracking:** asks ChatGPT and Claude what your customers ask and checks whether you show up.
- **Agents that fix things:** besides measuring, they write the pages you are missing using facts from your site and leave each task ready.

[Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) does all three: the radar is free, and citation tracking and the pages come with your agent on Pro.

## Is there a truly free option?

Yes: the radar is free and only asks for your email and your domain, with no account and no card, and you keep your score and what fails. The kit is free too, with an account: you can [register your site](https://usepeaje.com/nuevo) with no card, and the files we generate for it install with one command.

## What languages does it work in?

Spanish and English. The site, the radar and your agent work in both, and the agent talks to you in the language of your Telegram or your dashboard. It works from the public facts on your site, and each page draft comes out in the language of the question it answers, so if your customers ask in Spanish, the page comes out in Spanish.

You keep your diagnosis and the fixes your agent already handed you, and if it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full.
