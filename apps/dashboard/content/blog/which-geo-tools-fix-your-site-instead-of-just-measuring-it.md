# Which GEO tools fix your site instead of just measuring it?

Most GEO tools only measure: they tell you whether ChatGPT or Claude mention you, and their work ends there. The ones that fix leave you something to publish (a written page, a file, a change to your robots.txt), check it on your site once it is live and measure again weeks later. [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) is one of the second kind: the radar gives you the diagnosis for free and your agent hands you the fixes.

## How do I know whether a tool fixes or only measures?

Ask it these four questions before you pay:

1. **What does it hand me at the end of the week?** If the answer is a dashboard with a score, it measures. If it is a page written with your facts or a file ready to upload, it fixes.
2. **Who makes the change?** A tool that fixes leaves each change as a task with exact instructions, for you, for whoever runs your site or for your coding tool.
3. **Does it check that the change went live?** After you publish, it has to visit your site and confirm the change is there, without you asking.
4. **Does it measure again?** Without a second measurement against your starting point, you do not know whether the fix worked.

## What kinds of GEO tools are there?

| Kind | What it does | What it leaves you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radar or scanner | Checks your site and gives you a score with what is missing | A list of problems. The [Peaje radar](https://usepeaje.com/radar) is free and takes under a minute |
| Citation monitoring | Asks assistants what your customers ask and tells you whether they cite you | A number that goes up or down |
| Agent that measures and fixes | Diagnoses, writes the pages, leaves the tasks ready and measures again | Pages, files and finished tasks, with a date to measure again |
