# How do I hand Peaje's fixes to my developer without knowing code?

Each task Peaje leaves comes with instructions written for whoever will make the change, so you do not need to understand them. In your dashboard you tap "Copy for your AI", paste the text into a message to whoever runs your website (or into ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor, if you do it yourself) and, when they tell you it is published, you tap the button that says it is fixed. Peaje checks your live site and tells you whether it worked.

Updated: November 2026

The daily monitor and the tasks it leaves come with the free plan, according to the [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios). With Pro, your agent also writes fixes (8 credits each) and tasks can reach Claude Code or Cursor on their own.

## What does each task include?

Three parts, in plain words:

- **Why we suggest it:** what fails or is missing, and what you gain by fixing it.
- **What we expect:** what Peaje will check on your site when it is ready, for example that a page exists at a given address.
- **How to do it:** the instructions for whoever makes the change, with the exact text when needed.

With that, you decide whether it is worth it, and whoever runs your website knows what to do without asking you.

## How do I hand it to my developer?

1. Open the task in your dashboard and tap "Copy for your AI". Copy the full instructions.
2. Send them to whoever runs your site wherever you usually talk: WhatsApp, email or Slack.
3. When they tell you it is published, go back to the task and tap the button that marks it as fixed or published.
4. Peaje checks your site right then. If it does not see the change yet, the task stays "waiting verification" and Peaje checks again every day.

A message you can reuse:

> Hi, Peaje (the tool that checks how ChatGPT and Claude see us) left this fix for the site. The full instructions are below. Can you let me know when it is published? They check it on the live site on their own.
>
> [paste what you copied here]

## What if nobody runs my website?

If your site is built on an AI tool, like Lovable, Bolt or v0, or if you are up for Cursor or Claude Code, paste the instructions there: they are written for an AI to follow. With Pro, you can connect Claude Code, Cursor or Codex to Peaje once and then just tell it "apply Peaje's tasks": the tool reads the tasks, makes the changes and reports when they are published ([how to connect it](https://usepeaje.com/blog/how-do-i-send-my-peaje-tasks-to-claude-code-or-cursor-through-mcp)).

## How do I know it was done right without looking at the code?

You do not need to check it, because Peaje compares your published site with what "What we expect" says. Each task goes through these statuses:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Nobody has taken it |
| In progress | Someone is on it |
| Waiting verification | You were told it is done, and Peaje does not see it live yet |
| Verified | Peaje saw it on your published site |
| Dismissed | You decided it does not apply |

With Pro, when a new page is verified, Peaje measures again at 2 and 6 weeks whether ChatGPT and Claude cite it.

## What if a task makes no sense for my business?

Tap the button that says it does not work for you, and it is dismissed. That happens, for example, with a page about something you do not sell or a change your platform does not allow.
