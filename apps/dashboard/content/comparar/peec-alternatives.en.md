# Peec AI alternatives in 2026: five options with public prices

If Peec costs too much or does not track the engine you care about, there are five alternatives with public prices: Otterly is cheaper (€29 a month), AthenaHQ has a free plan and tracks Claude from its Starter plan, Semrush works if you already do your SEO there, Scrunch adds optimized and generated pages, and [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) is for a small business that needs the fixes done, though today it tracks only ChatGPT and Claude. If you have an SEO team working across several countries or projects, Peec is probably still your best option.

We read every price on the official pages on November 22, 2026. Peec starts at US$95 a month on Starter, according to its [pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing).

## Who should stay with Peec?

- Your team works across several countries or projects: Peec Pro includes 2 projects and 3 countries, and Advanced 5 projects.
- You run a store and want to see your products in shopping answers.
- You use Naver AI Briefing, ERNIE or Bing AI Mode, which the other tools on this list do not track.
- You need the whole team in: seats are unlimited on every plan.

## Why look for another one?

- Claude only comes with Peec's Enterprise plan.
- Starter costs US$95 a month and lets you pick 3 engines. Adding engines is paid separately.
- Peec recommends actions, but your team makes the changes to your site.

## The five alternatives

### Otterly

Lite is €29 a month before tax, with 16 prompts, 4 of 7 engines, a GEO audit and 3 recommendations a week. Standard (€189) goes up to 120 prompts and Premium (€489) to 403. Claude is among its seven engines, though each Claude check uses 3 credits. It fits if you want what Peec does for less money. [Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing).

### AthenaHQ

Essential is free, with 300 credits in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot. Starter is US$295 a month with 3,600 credits, 11 models (Claude included) and on-page and off-page actions. It fits if you want to try without paying or need Claude without going Enterprise. [AthenaHQ pricing](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing).

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

It costs US$99 a month per domain, with 25 prompts and no free trial. It tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, and adds Perplexity in Brand Performance. It does not track Claude as an assistant. It fits if you already pay for Semrush for SEO. [Peaje vs Semrush](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-semrush).

### Scrunch

Core is US$250 a month, with a 7-day free trial, 15,000 responses a month, 8 engines (no Claude), 5 seats and 10 pages optimized and 10 generated a month. It fits if you want to measure and also have pages optimized for you. [Scrunch pricing](https://scrunch.com/pricing).

### Peaje

The radar and the kit are free, and Pro is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses (US$69 after that). It tracks ChatGPT and Claude on 20 questions, writes up to 40 page drafts a month, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it. The limit: it does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or AI Overviews, it tracks fewer questions than Peec, and you, your developer or your coding tool publish the change once you say yes. It fits if you run a small business with no team. [Peaje vs Peec](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-peec).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest Peec alternative?

AthenaHQ has a free Essential plan with 300 credits. Among paid plans, Otterly Lite is €29 a month before tax and Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price. Peec Starter is US$95 a month. Prices read on November 22, 2026.

### Which Peec alternative tracks Claude?

Peaje tracks it on Pro, AthenaHQ from Starter, and Otterly has it among its seven engines. On Peec, Claude only comes with Enterprise.

### Is there a Peec alternative that makes the changes?

Scrunch generates and optimizes pages, and AthenaHQ has a content agent from Starter. Peaje writes the drafts, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it, and you publish the change once you say yes.

### Should I stay with Peec?

Yes, if your team works across several countries or projects, if you run a store with a catalog or if you use engines like Naver, ERNIE or Bing AI Mode. Peec has unlimited seats and more questions than Peaje from its entry plan.
