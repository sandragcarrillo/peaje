# Otterly alternatives in 2026: five options with public prices

Otterly Lite, at €29 a month, is one of the cheapest paid monitors, so most alternatives cost more. Switch if you need something else: Peec for several countries and projects, AthenaHQ to start free or get 11 models, Semrush if you already do your SEO there, Scrunch if you want optimized and generated pages, and [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) if you run a small business and what you are missing is someone to make the fixes, though today it tracks only ChatGPT and Claude.

We read every price on the official pages on November 22, 2026. Otterly charges in euros, before tax, according to its [pricing page](https://otterly.ai/pricing).

## Who should stay with Otterly?

- You want to track several engines on a small budget: Lite is €29 a month.
- Your team makes the changes and finds the GEO audit and recommendations useful.
- You run several brands or clients: from Standard up, workspaces are unlimited.
- You want your data over API, MCP or in Data Studio from Standard up.

## Why look for another one?

- Lite tracks 16 prompts and gives 3 recommendations a week.
- The card for each paid plan says it tracks 4 of 7 engines, and each Claude check uses 3 credits.
- Otterly audits and recommends, but your team makes the changes to your site.

## The five alternatives

### Peec

Starter is US$95 a month, with 50 prompts, 3 engines you pick, 1 project and 1 country. Pro (US$245) adds projects and countries. Claude only comes with Enterprise. It fits if you work across several countries or run a store with a catalog. [Peaje vs Peec](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-peec).

### AthenaHQ

Essential is free, with 300 credits in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot. Starter is US$295 a month with 3,600 credits, 11 models (Claude included) and on-page and off-page actions. It fits if you want to try without paying or need more models. [AthenaHQ pricing](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing).

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

It costs US$99 a month per domain, with 25 prompts and no free trial. It tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, and adds Perplexity in Brand Performance. It does not track Claude as an assistant. It fits if you already pay for Semrush for SEO. [Peaje vs Semrush](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-semrush).

### Scrunch

Core is US$250 a month, with a 7-day free trial, 15,000 responses a month, 8 engines (no Claude) and 10 pages optimized and 10 generated a month. It fits if you want the tool to work on your pages and you have the budget. [Scrunch pricing](https://scrunch.com/pricing).

### Peaje

The radar and the kit are free, and Pro is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses (US$69 after that), close to Otterly Lite. It tracks ChatGPT and Claude on 20 questions, writes up to 40 page drafts a month, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it. The limit: it does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or AI Overviews, the subscription is per site, and you, your developer or your coding tool publish the change once you say yes. It fits if you run a small business with no team. [Peaje vs Otterly](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-otterly).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a cheaper Otterly alternative?

AthenaHQ has a free Essential plan with 300 credits. Among paid plans, Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price, close to Otterly Lite at €29 before tax. The rest start at US$95 or more. Prices read on November 22, 2026.

### Which Otterly alternative fixes your site?

Scrunch optimizes and generates pages, and AthenaHQ has a content agent from Starter. Peaje writes the drafts, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it, and you publish the change once you say yes.

### Which Otterly alternative tracks more questions?

Peec Starter tracks 50 prompts for US$95 a month and Semrush 25 for US$99. Within Otterly, Standard goes up to 120 and Premium to 403. Peaje tracks 20.

### Should I stay with Otterly?

Yes, if you want several engines for little money and someone makes the changes, or if you run several brands: from Standard up, workspaces are unlimited.
