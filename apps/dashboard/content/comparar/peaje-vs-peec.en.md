# Peaje vs Peec AI: Peec tracks more engines, Peaje gets your fixes ready

If you have a marketing or SEO team that already ships changes to your website and you want to track your brand in Perplexity, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews, pick Peec: it tracks more engines and more questions. If you run a small business and nobody looks after the website, pick [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/): it prepares the fixes, checks they went live and measures again in ChatGPT and Claude. Peec starts at US$95 a month. Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price.

We read Peec's prices on its [pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing) on November 22, 2026.

## What is Peec AI?

Peec tracks how your brand shows up in the answers of AI assistants. You define a list of questions (they call them prompts) and Peec runs them every day to show you which answers mention you, which sources each assistant cites and where a competitor shows up instead of you.

Its plans, according to its pricing page:

- **Starter:** US$95 a month, 50 prompts, 1 project and 1 country.
- **Pro:** US$245 a month, 150 prompts, 2 projects and 3 countries.
- **Advanced:** US$495 a month, 350 prompts, 5 projects and 3 countries.
- **Enterprise:** custom pricing, billed yearly.

On Starter, Pro and Advanced you pick 3 engines from ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overview, AI Mode, Gemini, Naver AI Briefing, ERNIE and Bing AI Mode. Claude is only on Enterprise. Every plan includes unlimited seats, recommended actions, a gap analysis against your competitors and credits for its agent (500 on Starter). Paying yearly gets you 15% off, according to its FAQ.

## When is Peec the better choice?

- You care about showing up in Perplexity, Gemini, AI Mode or Google's AI Overviews. Peaje does not track them.
- You sell in several countries or run several brands. Peec includes projects and countries on each plan.
- You want to track 50 or more questions every day. Peaje tracks 20.
- Someone on your team already makes the changes to your site, and what you are missing is data.
- You work with a large team and need everyone in without paying per seat.

## What does Peaje not do?

Before you compare, here is what Peaje does not cover today:

- It tracks only ChatGPT and Claude. It does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Google's AI Overviews.
- It tracks 20 questions per site, not hundreds.
- The subscription is per site. If you have three brands, you pay three times.
- Your agent prepares each fix but does not change your site on its own. You, your developer or your coding tool publish it once you say yes.

## What does Peaje do that Peec's pages do not show?

Peec recommends actions, and Peaje stays with the work until the change is live on your site:

- It writes up to 40 page drafts a month from the facts on your site, and when a fact is missing it asks you instead of making it up.
- It leaves each fix as a task with acceptance criteria. Claude Code or Cursor pick it up over MCP or the CLI, or you hand it to whoever runs your website.
- When you mark the task done, it visits your live site and closes the task only if the change is published.
- It measures each page again at 2 and 6 weeks.
- It talks with you where you already work: the dashboard, Telegram, Slack or your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor).

## Who is each one for?

**Peaje** is for the owner of a small business who wants ChatGPT and Claude to recommend them and has nobody to turn a report into changes. The fixes arrive ready, and all the owner has to do is say yes.

**Peec** is for a team that already publishes and wants to measure more: more engines, more questions, several countries and several projects.

You can also use both: Peec for the engines Peaje does not track and Peaje for the fixes. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Peec AI cost compared with Peaje?

Peec starts at US$95 a month on its Starter plan, according to its pricing page read on November 22, 2026. Peaje Pro is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses that subscribe, and US$69 after that. Peaje's radar and kit are free.

### Does Peec track Claude?

Only on its Enterprise plan. On Starter, Pro and Advanced you pick 3 engines from ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overview, AI Mode, Gemini, Naver AI Briefing, ERNIE and Bing AI Mode. Peaje tracks ChatGPT and Claude on every site with Pro.

### Can I try it before paying?

With Peaje, the free radar checks your site in under a minute, with just your email and no card. Pro has no free trial, but if it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full. Peec shows a free trial button on its pricing page, though it does not say how long the trial lasts.

### Do I need a developer to use Peaje?

No. Your agent explains each fix in plain words and leaves it as a task. If you use Claude Code or Cursor, they pick it up from Peaje. If not, you hand it to whoever runs your website, and Peaje checks the live site afterwards.

### Does one subscription cover several sites?

On Peaje, no: the subscription is per site, according to the terms. On Peec, Starter includes 1 project, Pro includes 2 and Advanced includes 5.
