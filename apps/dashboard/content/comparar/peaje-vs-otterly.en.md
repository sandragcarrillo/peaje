# Peaje vs Otterly: Otterly tracks more engines for €29, Peaje prepares your fixes

If you want to see how you show up across several AI assistants and someone on your team makes the changes, Otterly Lite is one of the cheapest options: €29 a month for four engines, an audit of your site and three recommendations a week. If what you are missing is someone who writes the pages, gets each fix ready and checks it went live, pick [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/), which today tracks only ChatGPT and Claude. Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price, close to Otterly Lite.

We read Otterly's prices on its [pricing page](https://otterly.ai/pricing) on November 22, 2026. They are in euros and exclude tax.

## What is Otterly?

Otterly runs your questions (prompts) every day in AI assistants and shows you whether your brand appears, which sources get cited and how you compare with your competitors. It also includes a GEO audit that checks whether bots can read your site and how your content holds up.

Its plans, according to its pricing page:

- **Lite:** €29 a month, 16 prompts, 1 workspace and 3 recommendations a week.
- **Standard:** €189 a month, 120 prompts, unlimited workspaces, unlimited recommendations and API and MCP access.
- **Premium:** €489 a month and 403 prompts.
- **Enterprise:** custom, from 1,000 prompts.

There are seven engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, Gemini and Claude. The card for each paid plan says "4 of 7", though another part of the same page says every plan can track every engine, so if one engine matters to you, ask them before paying. Each Claude check uses 3 credits instead of 1. Every plan includes unlimited seats, yearly billing comes with 15% off, and there is a free trial, though the page does not say how long it lasts.

## When is Otterly the better choice?

- You want to track Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini or Google's AI Overviews. Peaje does not track them.
- Your budget is around €29 a month and you need measurement, because your team makes the changes.
- You want to track more than 20 questions: Standard goes up to 120 and Premium to 403.
- You run several brands or clients and need separate workspaces.
- You want your data over API, MCP or in Data Studio from Standard up.

## What does Peaje not do?

- It tracks only ChatGPT and Claude. It does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Google's AI Overviews.
- It tracks 20 questions per site.
- The subscription is per site, so each brand or client pays for its own.
- Your agent prepares each fix but does not change your site on its own. You, your developer or your coding tool publish it once you say yes.

## What does Peaje do that Otterly's pages do not show?

Otterly audits your site and recommends what to change, and Peaje covers the rest of the way:

- It writes up to 40 page drafts a month from the facts on your site, and when a fact is missing it asks you instead of making it up.
- It leaves each fix as a task with acceptance criteria. Claude Code or Cursor pick it up over MCP or the CLI, or you hand it to whoever runs your website.
- When you mark the task done, it visits your live site and closes the task only if the change is published.
- It measures each page again at 2 and 6 weeks to see whether it worked.
- It talks with you in the dashboard, on Telegram, on Slack or from your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor), and sends you a report every week.

## Who is each one for?

**Peaje** is for the owner who has neither the time nor the team to turn an audit into changes. The fixes arrive ready, the owner says yes and sees in the next measurement whether ChatGPT and Claude started naming them.

**Otterly** is for someone who already has hands to do the work and wants the cheapest monitor across several engines, or for an agency tracking many brands.

You can use both: Otterly for the engines Peaje does not track and Peaje for the fixes. ## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Otterly cost compared with Peaje?

Otterly Lite is €29 a month before tax, Standard €189 and Premium €489, according to its pricing page read on November 22, 2026. Peaje Pro is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses that subscribe, and US$69 after that. Peaje's radar and kit are free.

### Does Otterly track ChatGPT and Claude?

Otterly works with seven engines, ChatGPT and Claude among them, and the card for each paid plan says it tracks 4 of the 7. A Claude check uses 3 credits instead of 1. Peaje tracks ChatGPT and Claude on every site with Pro.

### Does Otterly fix my site?

Otterly audits your site and gives you recommendations, 3 a week on Lite and unlimited from Standard up. Its pricing page does not say it writes the pages or checks that the change went live. Peaje writes the drafts, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it.

### Can I try it before paying?

With Peaje, the free radar checks your site in under a minute, with just your email and no card. Pro has no free trial, but if it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full. Otterly offers a free trial on every plan, though its page does not say how long it lasts.

### Does one subscription cover several sites?

On Peaje, no: the subscription is per site, according to the terms. Otterly Lite includes 1 workspace, and from Standard up workspaces are unlimited.
