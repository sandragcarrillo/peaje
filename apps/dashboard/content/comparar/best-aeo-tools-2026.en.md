# Best AEO tools in 2026: seven options with public prices and when to pick each one

The best AEO tool depends on three things: which AI assistants matter to you, how many questions you want to track and who will make the changes to your site. If you have a team and want to track many engines, look at Peec, Otterly or Scrunch. If you are a large company, Profound. If you already live in Semrush, its AI Visibility Toolkit. If you want to start free, AthenaHQ has a no-cost plan. If you run a small business with no team and need the fixes done, Peaje, which today tracks only ChatGPT and Claude.

Peaje writes this list, and it is one of the seven. That is why each tool comes with when to pick it, and Peaje also says when not to. We read every price on the official pages on November 22, 2026.

## How do I choose?

- **Which engines matter to you.** If your customers ask Gemini or find you through Google's AI Overviews, rule out the tools that do not track them, Peaje included.
- **How many questions.** Peaje tracks 20, Otterly Lite 16, Semrush 25 and Peec Starter 50. If you need hundreds, go to Otterly Premium, Peec Advanced or an Enterprise plan.
- **Who makes the changes.** Almost all of them tell you what to change. If nobody in your business will do it, look for one that gets the fix ready and checks it went live.
- **How many sites or brands.** Peaje charges per site, Semrush per domain, Peec per project, and Otterly includes unlimited workspaces from Standard up.
- **How you want to buy.** Profound asks for a demo and a contract. The rest you can buy by card from their pages.

## The seven, one by one

### AthenaHQ

Its Essential plan is free, with 300 credits (1 credit is 1 AI response), unlimited users and content recommendations in ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini and Copilot. Starter is US$295 a month, with 3,600 credits, 11 models (Claude included) and on-page and off-page actions. Paying yearly gets you 17% off. Pick it if you want to try without paying or need many engines with an agent that optimizes content. Skip it if 300 credits fall short and US$295 a month does not fit your budget.

### Otterly

Lite is €29 a month before tax, with 16 prompts, a GEO audit and 3 recommendations a week. Standard (€189) goes up to 120 prompts and Premium (€489) to 403. The card for each plan says it tracks 4 of 7 engines. Pick it if you want the cheapest monitor with several engines and someone makes the changes. Skip it if nobody will turn its recommendations into changes. [Peaje vs Otterly](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-otterly).

### Peaje

The radar and the kit are free, and Pro is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses (US$69 after that), with 500 credits a month. Your agent writes up to 40 page drafts a month, leaves each fix as a task, checks your live site and measures again at 2 and 6 weeks. Pick it if you run a small business with no team and want ChatGPT and Claude to recommend you. Skip it if you need Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or AI Overviews, want to track more than 20 questions or are after classic SEO: Peaje does none of that today.

### Peec

Starter is US$95 a month, with 50 prompts, 3 engines you pick, 1 project and 1 country. Pro (US$245) and Advanced (US$495) add prompts, projects and countries. Claude only comes with Enterprise. Pick it if you have an SEO team, a store with a catalog or brands in several countries. Skip it if Claude is your main engine and you will not pay for Enterprise. [Peaje vs Peec](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-peec).

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

It costs US$99 a month per domain, with 25 prompts and no free trial, and each extra user is another US$99 license. It also comes inside Semrush One, from US$199 a month. Pick it if you already work on your SEO in Semrush and want AI visibility in the same place. Skip it if Claude matters to you, because it does not track it as an assistant. [Peaje vs Semrush](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-semrush).

### Scrunch

Core is US$250 a month, billed monthly, with a 7-day free trial, 15,000 responses a month, 8 engines, 5 seats and 1 brand. It includes 10 pages optimized and 10 generated a month. Claude is an Enterprise add-on. Pick it if you want to track many engines and also optimize pages, and the price works for you. Skip it if you need Claude without paying for Enterprise.

### Profound

It only sells custom Enterprise plans, through a demo. It retired its Starter and Growth plans in September 2026. It tracks up to 10 engines and comes with AI Marketer and agents for your marketing team. Pick it if you are a large company with a yearly budget. Skip it if you run a small business: there is no plan for you. [Peaje vs Profound](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-profound).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest AEO tool in 2026?

AthenaHQ has a free Essential plan with 300 credits. Among paid plans, Otterly Lite is €29 a month before tax and Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price. Peaje also has a free radar and kit. Prices read on November 22, 2026.

### Which AEO tool tracks Claude?

Of these seven, Peaje and AthenaHQ Starter track Claude on their entry paid plan, and Otterly lists it among its seven engines. Peec keeps it for Enterprise, Scrunch offers it as an Enterprise add-on and Profound includes it in its custom plan. The Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit does not track it as an assistant.

### Which AEO tool fixes your site instead of just measuring it?

Almost all of them recommend changes. AthenaHQ (from Starter) and Scrunch optimize or generate pages, and Profound has agents for your marketing team. Peaje writes the drafts, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it, though you, your developer or your coding tool publish the change.

### Do I need an AEO tool if I already use Semrush?

If you have a team and measuring is enough, the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit may cover you for US$99 a month. If what you are missing is someone to make the changes, or Claude matters to you, it is worth adding a tool that prepares them.
