# Profound alternatives in 2026: six options, with and without an Enterprise contract

Since September 2026 Profound only sells custom Enterprise plans, through a demo. If you want another large platform, Peec, Scrunch, AthenaHQ and Otterly have their own Enterprise plans plus plans you can buy by card from US$95, US$250, US$295 and €29 a month. If you already work in Semrush, its AI Visibility Toolkit is US$99 a month. If you run a small business and need the fixes done, [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) is US$30 a month at the founder price, though today it tracks only ChatGPT and Claude.

We read every price on the official pages on November 22, 2026. Profound retired its Starter (US$399) and Growth (US$499) plans for new subscriptions, according to its [pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing).

## Who should stay with Profound?

- You already work with Profound and need engines like DeepSeek, Exa, Muse Spark or Qwen. On this list, outside Profound, only Peec Enterprise covers them.
- Your marketing team will use AI Marketer and Profound's agents for its recurring work.
- You want AI bot analytics on unlimited domains, connected to your infrastructure.
- Your company already buys through a demo, a contract and a yearly budget.

## Why look for another one?

- There is no public price and no plan you can buy by card.
- If you were on Starter or Growth, those prices are gone for new subscriptions.
- For a small business, an Enterprise contract is more than it needs.

## The six alternatives

### Peec

Starter is US$95 a month, with 50 prompts and 3 engines you pick. Its Enterprise plan, billed yearly, has unlimited prompts and up to 26 models, Claude, DeepSeek and Qwen among them, plus API and SSO. It fits if you want an Enterprise plan with as many models as Profound or more, or to start by card. [Peec pricing](https://peec.ai/pricing).

### Scrunch

Core is US$250 a month, with a 7-day free trial, 8 engines and 10 pages optimized and 10 generated a month. Its Enterprise plan, billed yearly, adds unlimited seats and brands, Agent Pages, API, SSO and Claude as an add-on. It fits if you want to measure and also have the tool work on your pages. [Scrunch pricing](https://scrunch.com/pricing).

### AthenaHQ

Essential is free with 300 credits. Starter is US$295 a month with 3,600 credits, 11 models (Claude and DeepSeek included) and on-page and off-page actions. Its Enterprise plan has custom credits, role-based access and SSO. It fits if you want many models without a yearly contract. [AthenaHQ pricing](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing).

### Otterly

Lite is €29 a month before tax and Premium €489 with 403 prompts. Its Enterprise plan starts at 1,000 prompts, with SSO and monthly sessions with a GEO expert. It fits if you want many prompts at a published price. [Otterly pricing](https://otterly.ai/pricing).

### Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

It costs US$99 a month per domain, with 25 prompts, and tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Perplexity. It does not track Claude as an assistant. Semrush also has an Enterprise AI optimization solution with custom pricing. It fits if your team already does its SEO in Semrush. [Peaje vs Semrush](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-semrush).

### Peaje

The radar and the kit are free, and Pro is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses (US$69 after that), with no demo or contract. It tracks ChatGPT and Claude on 20 questions, writes up to 40 page drafts a month, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site before closing it. The limit: it does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek or AI Overviews, it has no Enterprise plan, and you, your developer or your coding tool publish the change once you say yes. It fits if you run a small business with no team. [Peaje vs Profound](https://usepeaje.com/compare/peaje-vs-profound).

## Frequently asked questions

### What happened to Profound's Starter and Growth plans?

Profound retired them in September 2026. According to its pricing page, read on November 22, 2026, the US$399 and US$499 prices are no longer available for new subscriptions, and only the custom Enterprise plan remains.

### What is the cheapest Profound alternative?

AthenaHQ has a free Essential plan with 300 credits. Among paid plans, Otterly Lite is €29 a month before tax and Peaje Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price, both with no contract.

### Which Profound alternative tracks as many engines?

Peec Enterprise goes up to 26 models, and AthenaHQ Starter tracks 11, Claude included, for US$295 a month. Scrunch Core tracks 8 for US$250 a month, without Claude.

### Should I stay with Profound?

Yes, if your company needs engines like DeepSeek, Exa, Muse Spark or Qwen (on this list only Peec Enterprise also covers them), if your marketing team will use its agents and if you already buy on a yearly contract.
