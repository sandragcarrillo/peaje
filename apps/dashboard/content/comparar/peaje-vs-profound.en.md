# Peaje vs Profound: Profound is for companies with an Enterprise budget, Peaje for a small business

If you are a large company with a marketing team and budget for a custom contract, Profound tracks up to 10 AI engines and comes with agents that work for your team. If you run a small business, Profound no longer has a plan for you: since September 2026 it only sells Enterprise. [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/) is US$30 a month at the founder price, tracks ChatGPT and Claude and leaves each fix ready to publish.

We read Profound's details on its [pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) on November 22, 2026.

## What is Profound?

Profound is an AI visibility platform for companies. Every day it tracks the questions you agree on with them across up to 10 engines: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, DeepSeek, Claude, Google AI Overviews, Exa, Muse Spark and Qwen.

What its pricing page shows:

- **A single plan, Enterprise,** with custom packages. To get a price you request a demo.
- **Its Starter and Growth plans were retired in September 2026.** The US$399 and US$499 prices are no longer available for new subscriptions, according to its FAQ.
- **AI Marketer,** which runs on custom-priced credits, and agents to build and reuse your marketing team's recurring workflows.
- **Agent analytics** with unlimited domains and custom integrations with platforms such as Cloudflare and Vercel.

## When is Profound the better choice?

- You need to track many engines at once, including DeepSeek, Qwen or Exa.
- You have a large marketing team that will use its agents to research and write content.
- You want AI bot analytics connected to your infrastructure across several domains.
- Your company buys through a demo, a contract and a yearly budget, and that is fine.

## What does Peaje not do?

- It tracks only ChatGPT and Claude. It does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, DeepSeek or Google's AI Overviews.
- It tracks 20 questions per site, while Profound sizes them to your case.
- It has no Enterprise plans, custom contracts or integrations with your infrastructure.
- Your agent prepares each fix but does not change your site on its own. You, your developer or your coding tool publish it once you say yes.

## What does Peaje do for a small business?

- You can start today without a demo: the free radar checks your site in under a minute, and Pro is bought from the [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios).
- It writes up to 40 page drafts a month from the facts on your site, and when a fact is missing it asks you instead of making it up.
- It leaves each fix as a task with acceptance criteria. Claude Code or Cursor pick it up over MCP or the CLI, or you hand it to whoever runs your website.
- It checks your live site before closing each task and measures the page again at 2 and 6 weeks.
- It talks with you in the dashboard, on Telegram, on Slack or from your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor).

## Who is each one for?

**Peaje** is for the owner of a small business who wants ChatGPT and Claude to recommend them, without a yearly contract or a demo, and who needs the fixes done more than another dashboard.

**Profound** is for a large company that wants to track many engines, put agents to work with its marketing team and negotiate a contract.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does Profound cost?

Profound does not publish a price. Its pricing page, read on November 22, 2026, shows a single custom Enterprise plan and asks you to book a demo. Its Starter and Growth plans, at US$399 and US$499, were retired for new subscriptions in September 2026.

### Is there a Profound alternative for a small business?

Peaje is US$30 a month for the first 50 businesses that subscribe, and US$69 after that, and its radar is free. It tracks ChatGPT and Claude, not Profound's 10 engines, and in exchange it writes the pages, leaves each fix as a task and checks your live site.

### Does Profound track Claude?

Yes. Claude is among the 10 engines listed on its pricing page, along with ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, DeepSeek, Google AI Overviews, Exa, Muse Spark and Qwen.

### Can I try it before paying?

With Peaje, the free radar checks your site in under a minute, with just your email and no card. Pro has no free trial, but if it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full. Profound asks you to book a demo.

### Do I need a developer to use Peaje?

No. Your agent explains each fix in plain words and leaves it as a task. If you use Claude Code or Cursor, they pick it up from Peaje. If not, you hand it to whoever runs your website, and Peaje checks the live site afterwards.
