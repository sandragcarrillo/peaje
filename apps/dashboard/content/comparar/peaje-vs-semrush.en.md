# Peaje vs Semrush AI Visibility: which one does my small business need?

If you already use Semrush for SEO and someone makes the changes, the AI Visibility Toolkit tracks you in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode and Google's AI Overviews for US$99 a month. If you run a small business with no technical team and what you are missing is someone who tells you what to change and gets it ready, pick [Peaje](https://usepeaje.com/): Pro is US$30 a month at the founder price (US$69 after the first 50 businesses) and includes 40 page drafts a month written from your site's facts, according to the [pricing page](https://usepeaje.com/precios). Today Peaje tracks only ChatGPT and Claude.

We read Semrush's details in its [help center](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) and its [guide to AI visibility features](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1626-ai-visibility-features) on November 22, 2026.

## What is the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit?

It is Semrush's package for seeing how you show up in AI answers. According to its help center, it costs US$99 a month and includes:

- 1 project and 1 domain for the Brand Performance report.
- 25 prompts in Prompt Tracking.
- 300 daily queries in the AI Analysis reports and 1,000 in Prompt Research.
- AI search checks in Site Audit, up to 100 pages.

Each extra user is another US$99 license, 50 more prompts cost US$60 a month and each extra domain is US$99 a month. The toolkit has no free trial. It also comes inside Semrush One, which starts at US$199 a month and bundles the SEO and AI tools.

Which engines it covers depends on the report. The Visibility Overview tracks ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and Google's AI Overviews, and Brand Performance adds Perplexity. Claude shows up in its guide only as a bot checked in the site audit, not as an assistant being tracked.

On changes, Brand Performance gives you AI-generated strategic recommendations and Site Audit flags what blocks bots. None of its pages say the toolkit writes or publishes content for you.

## When is Semrush the better choice?

- You already pay for Semrush for SEO and want everything in one place.
- You care about showing up in Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Mode or AI Overviews. Peaje does not track them.
- You have a marketing team or a developer who turns reports into changes.
- You want to cross AI visibility with your keywords, your traffic and your competitors on Google.

## What does Peaje not do?

- It tracks only ChatGPT and Claude. It does not track Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Google's AI Overviews.
- It does not do classic SEO: it does not track Google keywords or analyze backlinks.
- It tracks 20 questions per site, and the subscription is per site.
- Your agent prepares each fix but does not change your site on its own. You, your developer or your coding tool publish it once you say yes.

## Who is each option for?

The AI Visibility Toolkit is a measuring tool. It is built for marketing teams that already have someone to make the changes and want one more report inside their Semrush suite.

Peaje is for a small business without that team. Your agent tells you what is missing, hands you the fix ready and checks it once it is published:

- It writes up to 40 page drafts a month from the facts on your site, and when a fact is missing it asks you instead of making it up.
- It leaves each fix as a task with acceptance criteria. Claude Code or Cursor pick it up over MCP or the CLI, or you hand it to whoever runs your website.
- It visits your live site before closing each task and measures the page again at 2 and 6 weeks.
- It talks with you in the dashboard, on Telegram, on Slack or from your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor).

## Which one do I need for my case?

- **Small business with no technical team:** Peaje. Your agent explains each fix in plain words and leaves it as a task, and if you use Claude Code or Cursor, they pick it up from Peaje.
- **Marketing team with its own developer that already uses Semrush:** the AI Visibility Toolkit may be enough to measure, though the fixes are still your team's work.
- **You care about Gemini or Google's AI Overviews:** Semrush, because Peaje does not track them. You can add Peaje for the fixes in ChatGPT and Claude.
- **You only want to know where you stand today:** start with the [free radar](https://usepeaje.com/radar), which takes under a minute and only asks for your email.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does the Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit cost?

US$99 a month per domain, according to Semrush's help center read on November 22, 2026. It includes 25 prompts, 50 more cost US$60 a month, and each extra user is another US$99 license. There is no free trial. Semrush One, which includes it along with the SEO tools, starts at US$199 a month.

### Does Semrush AI Visibility track Claude?

Not as an assistant. Its features guide lists ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews and Perplexity, and Claude appears only as a bot in the site audit. Peaje tracks ChatGPT and Claude.

### Can I try Peaje without paying?

Yes. The radar and the kit are free and need no card. You see your score today, what fails and how far it can go. You can [register your site](https://usepeaje.com/nuevo), and nothing changes until you decide. You only pay if you want the agent.

### Does Peaje have a yearly plan?

No. Pro is billed every month in dollars, by card, and renews on its own until you cancel. You keep the US$30 founder price while your subscription stays active.

### What happens if I cancel or run out of credits?

Cancel from your dashboard, with no calls. You keep Pro until the end of the month you already paid for, and what your agent handed you stays in your dashboard. If it is your first payment and you ask within 7 days, we refund it in full. If you spend the 500 credits before the month ends, your agent keeps talking with you but does no new work until the 1st, and nothing extra is charged.

### How many sites does one subscription cover?

One. A Peaje subscription is per site, according to the terms. You can register several sites with the same account and each one gets its own score, but each site on Pro pays its own subscription. On Semrush, each extra domain for Brand Performance costs US$99 a month.
